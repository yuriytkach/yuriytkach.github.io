Report for Night Vision Device Fundraiser 📝

May 19, 2026

← All reports

Thank you to everyone who contributed to our fundraiser for a night vision device for a soldier of the 48th Separate Reconnaissance Battalion «Тиха ніч» (Silent Night)! Your support once again made a real difference for our defenders on the front lines.

This fundraiser was a true team effort — my supporters from YouTube and Telegram, my friends, my family, and my own contribution all came together to make it happen. Every donation, big or small, helped us reach the goal.

📊 Some Numbers:

  • Total raised: nearly 50,000 UAH

The night vision device has already been purchased and recently delivered to our soldier. Attached is a photo of him with the new equipment, which will help him stay safe and effective during nighttime reconnaissance missions.

From myself and from our defender, I want to extend a heartfelt thank you to everyone who joined this effort — by donating, sharing, or supporting in any way. Your help truly matters!

Together to victory! 💪 Glory to Ukraine! 🇺🇦 Слава Україні! 🇺🇦
